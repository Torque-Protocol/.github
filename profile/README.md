# TORQUE

Knock-out leverage on NVDA, settled in USDG, live on Robinhood Chain mainnet.

**The vault is never short.** Every position is bought in full as real NVDA and held by the contract, so LPs lend against real collateral instead of taking the other side of the trade, and the most a trader can lose is their margin.

**The gap.** At Robinhood Chain block 78,677,903 there was $700.7M of USDG on the chain, but only $1.51M of it was lent against stock tokens, and 96.6% of that was already borrowed.

**Status.** Live on Robinhood Chain mainnet since block 78,466,999: [TorqueMarket](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/address/0xbee6Da89F879B018Fea5d7A78311db720B9D8096) and [TorqueVault](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/address/0xb4dBEF56F9E93ED9A7649E065252dc98013B96Bf), both an exact bytecode match on Sourcify, with no owner, no pause switch and no upgrade path. The LP vault is capped at $20 USDG for the buildathon. Not audited.
